# Investment & Partnership Proposal: Metroflow

## 1. Executive Summary
This document outlines the investment opportunity for Metroflow, a scalable, feature-rich project management and collaboration SaaS platform. Built with modern technology (Node.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL), Metroflow is positioned to capture a significant share of the growing productivity tools market in Africa and beyond. We are seeking a strategic partner or investor to accelerate growth or acquire the technology outright.

## 2. The Opportunity
The demand for remote work tools and efficient team management software is at an all-time high. Metroflow addresses this market with a unique blend of **Task Management**, **Team Gamification**, and **Innovation Tracking**. Unlike generic competitors, Metroflow is designed to keep teams engaged and motivated.

**Key Technical Assets:**
*   **Robust Backend:** Built on Node.js/Express with TypeScript for type safety and scalability.
*   **Enterprise-Grade Database:** PostgreSQL schema designed for multi-tenancy and high performance.
*   **Scalable Architecture:** Modular codebase (Controllers, Services, Routes) ready for microservices migration.
*   **Integrated Payments:** Built-in subscription handling and payment gateway integration readiness.

## 3. Investment Options
We are open to two primary modes of partnership: **Outright Acquisition** or **Strategic Investment**.

For investors looking to own a complete, deployable SaaS product with full intellectual property rights. This includes the entire codebase, documentation, and deployment scripts.

*   **Proposed Valuation:** **₦15,000,000 - ₦25,000,000 (NGN)**
*   **What You Get:**
    *   100% Source Code Ownership (Backend & Database Schema).
    *   Full Intellectual Property Rights (Transfer of Copyright).
    *   3 Months of Technical Handover & Support.
    *   Zero Royalty/Equity retained by developers.
*   **Rationale:** This amount covers the estimated 1,500+ hours of senior engineering development time, architectural design, and immediate go-to-market readiness. It is significantly lower than the cost of building a similar enterprise-grade system from scratch.

### Option B: Strategic Investment (Equity Partnership)
For investors who want to partner with the current team to scale the product, handle marketing, and drive user acquisition.

*   **Investment Ask:** **₦5,000,000 - ₦10,000,000 (NGN)**

    *   **30% Product Development:** Mobile app development and AI feature integration.

*   **Projected ROI:**

    *   **Year 3:** 5x - 10x return on investment through dividends or Series A exit.
